Suggestions arrive earlier: every learned word competes from the first letter, longer words gain from the letters they save, and inflected forms of the same word strengthen each other. A word typed only once no longer gets the own-word bonus, so typos stop clogging the row. Ignored suggestions sink in proportion to their use and return as soon as you type them. Sentence starts are learned: after a full stop or in an empty field, your usual opening words are offered.
